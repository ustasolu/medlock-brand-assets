Privacy Policy

Medlock Pharmacy ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information in connection with our website, social media accounts, and the internal tools we use to manage our online presence.

2. Our Use of the Meta Platform

We use the Meta (Facebook and Instagram) API exclusively to publish and manage our own pharmacy's social media content. Specifically, our application:

  • Posts content to our own Medlock Pharmacy Facebook Page
  • Posts content to our own Medlock Pharmacy Instagram Business account
  • Reads engagement data (likes, comments, reach) on posts we have published
  • Geo-tags our posts with our pharmacy location

We do not collect, store, or process the personal data of any Facebook or Instagram users. Our app access is limited to managing Medlock Pharmacy's own business accounts. No user data from the Meta platforms is transmitted to or stored by Medlock Pharmacy.

3. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information, and only what is necessary to operate our pharmacy and provide you with the best care:

  • Pharmacy customers: Name, date of birth, prescription information, insurance details, and contact information as required to dispense medications and provide clinical services. This information is protected under HIPAA.
  • Website visitors: We may collect anonymized analytics data (pages visited, referral source) to improve our site. We do not use advertising trackers or sell any data.
  • Social media followers: If you contact us via Facebook, Instagram, or Google, your message and public profile name are visible to us. We do not store this data in third-party systems.

4. How We Use Information

  • To fill your prescriptions and coordinate your healthcare
  • To communicate appointment reminders, refill notifications, and health information
  • To respond to your questions and messages
  • To publish educational and community content on our social media pages
  • To comply with applicable laws, including HIPAA and Georgia pharmacy regulations

5. HIPAA — Your Health Information

As a pharmacy, we are a HIPAA-covered entity. All protected health information (PHI) is handled in accordance with our Notice of Privacy Practices, which is available at our pharmacy counter and upon request. We never share your health information on social media or in any marketing communications without your explicit written consent.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share information only:

  • With healthcare providers involved in your care (as permitted by HIPAA)
  • With your insurance company to process claims
  • When required by law or a valid legal process

7. Data Retention

Prescription and health records are retained for a minimum of 10 years as required by Georgia law. Social media post content is retained indefinitely as part of our business records. We do not retain personal data of social media users beyond the scope of direct communications.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data, where permitted by law
  • Opt out of any marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.

9. Data Deletion Request

If you have interacted with Medlock Pharmacy through a Meta platform and wish to request deletion of any data we may hold, please email us at Info@medlockrx.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request." We will respond within 30 days and confirm deletion of any applicable data.

10. Cookies

Our website may use cookies for basic functionality and analytics. We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking. You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting your ability to view our site.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of our services after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
